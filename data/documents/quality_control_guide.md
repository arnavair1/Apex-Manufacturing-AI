# Manufacturing Quality Control Guide

## Quality Inspections

Quality inspections are used to identify defects and monitor manufacturing quality.

Each inspection should record:

- Machine
- Product
- Inspection date
- Whether a defect was found
- Defect type
- Quality score

## Defect Investigation

When a defect is detected:

1. Identify the affected product.
2. Identify the machine that produced it.
3. Review production conditions.
4. Review recent machine failures.
5. Review maintenance history.
6. Inspect the production process.
7. Record corrective action.

## Quality Score

Quality scores should be monitored over time.

A declining quality score may indicate a change in:

- Machine condition
- Production process
- Raw materials
- Operator conditions
- Maintenance status

## Corrective Action

Corrective actions should be documented and linked to the affected machine or production process whenever possible.

## Quality Monitoring

Manufacturing teams should monitor:

- Total inspections
- Total defects
- Defect rate
- Average quality score
- Defects by machine
- Defects by product
- Defects over time
